wordpress.org / museum

Twenty-four ways to see a history

WordPress history as desktops, songs, artifacts, signals, profiles, photos, paper, and living worlds. Pick a door.

Exhibit I

Desktop Museum

Boot into a Windows XP desktop where every version is a shortcut, the Start menu is a time machine, and the taskbar keeps ticking.
Exhibit II

Winamp Timeline

A skeumorphic music player where every release is a track. Cue up 2003, drop the needle, and the Playground picks up the beat.
Exhibit III

Scrapbook Time Machine

A handmade scrapbook you scroll through — polaroids that develop into live Playgrounds, handwritten margin notes, wax seals, coffee rings.
Exhibit IV

Kubrick Weblog

A period-accurate early-WordPress blog where every release is a post. Click the Dashboard to open a working fake wp-admin: draggable widgets, color schemes, QuickPress, and the Hello Dolly plugin.
Exhibit V · scrollytelling

Horizontal Magnetic

Scroll left-to-right through fifty-three full-viewport slides. Parallax portraits, chiptune that shifts key per decade, and a ribbon that snaps to any release.
Exhibit VI · scrollytelling

Pinned Storytelling

Each release pins to the screen while it unfolds in four beats — portrait drawing in, trivia surfacing, screenshot breathing, Playground booting. Music layers features as the product does.
Exhibit VII · 3D

The Jazz Corridor

A first-person walk through a 3D CSS museum. Every frame a release, every plaque a codename portrait. Step into any painting and the Playground fills the room.
Exhibit VIII · Deep Dive

The Impossible Museum

The corridor breaks open. You jump, fall, shrink, walk the ceiling, and drift through nine releases as gravity and scale rewrite themselves between rooms. Each stop zooms in — pixel portraits, lost screenshots, deeper trivia.
Exhibit IX · Open World

The Lost Cartography

No more straight line. A sprawling maze of wings, gardens, vaults, and rooftops linked by unexpected shortcuts. Camera angles change with the terrain — overhead for the courtyard, cinematic tilt for the descent, first-person for the archives. Wander and you will find releases you didn't expect to meet here.
Exhibit X · The Masterpiece

Nine Movements

Each release stops being a room you pass and becomes a movement in a suite. Approach, anticipation, arrival, a sustained moment with its own camera language, then departure. The scroll keeps doing something the whole time — parallax, orbit, focus-pull — so even standing still feels alive. Built as a presentation, not a demo.
Exhibit XI · The Artifact

Raiders of the Release

The walls hold still now. You round corners, leap a chasm, climb a rope, duck into a cave, outrun a boulder, watch a stone door grind open onto a pyramid — and there, as an artifact on the plinth, sits the release. Each one stops the journey and fills the screen: the screenshot, the musician, the story. Then you move on.
Exhibit XII · The Expedition

The Long Way Through

Each level opens up: you take the long way through it before you find the artifact. When you do, you don't get a popup — the release itself in the world grows forward, fills the screen, and the level continues breathing behind it. Corners, caves, ropes, bridges, boulders, a cathedral, a moonlit garden — the journey stops being a corridor and starts being a place.
Exhibit XIII · Release Odyssey

The Playable Archive

A scroll-controlled multi-level game of separate release worlds: jump across the Miles mountain lake, crawl through Mingus underground ducts, swim past Strayhorn fish and bubbles, fall through Duke machinery, fly over Thelonious city skies, slide through Benny media towers, and ride the Bebo block foundry into a live Playground portal.
Exhibit XIV · Object Worlds

Memory Objects

RSS tiles, plugin plugs, theme swatches, comment bubbles, and blocks gain weight and personality. Six verbs replace the release timeline; each object opens a different WordPress memory.
Exhibit XV · Flash Futurism

Signal Lab

A gunmetal-and-cyan instrument receives five transmissions from WordPress history. Tune the dial, patch the REST circuit, decode the signal, then route one live build to Playground.
Exhibit XVI · Theme Lab

The Theme Lab

One immutable post wears eight radically different designs. Switch eras, inspect the shared structure, and watch the theme system change clothes without changing the words underneath.
Exhibit XVII · Social Web

The Profile Wall

WordPress gets the gloriously overdecorated profile it deserved: plugins become Top Friends, themes become outfits, contributors fill the wall, and the guestbook is waiting for your signature.
Exhibit XVIII · Poster Film

The Codename Sessions

Eight jazz codenames become eight saturated silhouette cuts joined by one unbroken white cable. Scroll, tap, or play the kinetic poster sequence straight through.
Exhibit XIX · Pocket Chat

PictoPress

A friendly dual-screen appliance for four rooms of WordPress history. Draw a note, stamp it, send it, and watch contributors from another era answer in pixels.
Exhibit XX · Box Archaeology

Shrinkwrap

WordPress was never sold in a box, so the museum manufactured the stockroom that never existed. Unwrap manuals, discs, receipts, installer cards, and nine different stories from the software aisle.
Exhibit XXI · Folksonomy

Everyone's Archive

Twenty-eight hand-drawn snapshots, seventeen human tags, and no timeline. Every tag rearranges the archive into a different composition: contact sheet, salon wall, map, light table, cluster, or annotated photo.
Exhibit XXII · Gutenberg Zine

Paste-Up

WordPress 5.0 is told as torn paper, loud type, stickers, arguments, and a workbench where scraps snap into blocks. Assembly is not decoration here; it is the entire point.
Exhibit XXIII · Living System

The Publishing Terrarium

A glossy Frutiger Aero ecosystem where posts grow, comments pollinate, RSS bubbles rise, and updates change the weather. Tend the system until today's WordPress condenses inside a dewdrop.
Exhibit XXIV · Release Arcade

Patchwork Arcade

Ten WordPress design shifts collide with ten screen styles from their moment. Read the release table, play each tiny period game, and stamp the years into one growing collage.
Curated with care. Each exhibit loads live WordPress instances through WordPress Playground.
